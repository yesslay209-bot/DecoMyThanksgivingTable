# Code of Conduct

DecoThanks is about gratitude, and we want working on it to feel the same way.

**Do:** be welcoming to newcomers, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback on code, and credit other people's work.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post sexual or violent content; share anyone's private information; or keep pushing a point after being asked to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
